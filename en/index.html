Kruzzle — Privacy Policy

Publisher: LatenLukken Studio Contact: info@latenlukken.com Effective date: 2026-10-05 This policy: https://furkanuruk.github.io/kruzzle-privacy/

Kruzzle is a crossword game for learning languages. This policy explains what data the app and its service providers process, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Summary

  • Learning progress stays on your device. Words, stars, coins, streaks and achievements are stored in a local database on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • Ads (Google AdMob). The app shows rewarded and interstitial ads. Google processes device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and (with consent where required) personalise ads.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • No accounts, no own server. Kruzzle has no sign-in and we do not operate a backend; we never ask for your name, email or phone number.

2. Data we process

2.1 Stored only on your device (not collected by us)

Learning progress, puzzle results, coin balance, streaks, achievements, settings, lives, and the IDs of purchases the app has already processed. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2.2 Advertising — Google AdMob

When ads are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google, according to Google's own disclosure (https://support.google.com/admob/answer/11952882): - Device or other identifiers: the Android Advertising ID / app set ID, or on iOS the IDFA (only if you allow tracking) and the IDFV; - Approximate location derived from your IP address; - App interactions with ads (views, taps, rewards earned); - Diagnostics (SDK performance and crash information).

Purpose: showing ads, measuring them, preventing fraud, and — only where you consent or where permitted by law — personalising ads. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and can change their choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the app.

2.3 Purchases — Google Play Billing / Apple App Store

Products: coin packs (coins_300, coins_1000, coins_3000). Payment is processed by Google or Apple under their terms; they share with the app only the product identifier and a purchase/transaction identifier, which are kept locally to grant the item and avoid granting it twice. We do not run a server that receives your purchases.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Processing Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Personalised ads, ad-related device storage/access Consent (6(1)(a)), collected via Google's consent form
Non-personalised ads, ad fraud prevention and measurement Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) in funding a free app, subject to the ePrivacy consent collected by the same form
Purchases Performance of a contract (6(1)(b)); processed by Google/Apple as independent controllers

5. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their standard contractual clauses and certifications.

6. Retention

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Ad data: retained by Google as described in its policies.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google/Apple as described in their policies.

7. Deleting your data

  • On-device data: uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Kruzzle → Clear data.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS, or change consent in Settings → Ad privacy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA), you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact info@latenlukken.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Kruzzle is not directed to children under 13, and its recommended target audience on Google Play is 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. Security

All network traffic (AdMob, stores) is encrypted in transit with TLS.

11. Changes

We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
